# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss the work, explain disagreements, and give actionable review
feedback. Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

The maintainer may remove abusive content and limit participation after reviewing the context.
Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security/contact process; do not post
private details in public issues. These expectations apply in issues, PRs and project discussions.
